# Operator Training Manual

**Course Code:** WH-TRN-OP-001
**Duration:** 4 hours
**Prerequisites:** General warehouse safety orientation

## Module 1: Introduction to AutoStore

### What is AutoStore?
AutoStore is a cubic storage system where bins containing inventory are stored in a grid and retrieved by robots. As an operator, you work at a workstation (called a "port") where bins are automatically delivered to you for picking, replenishment, or returns processing.

### Key Terminology
- **Grid:** The aluminum framework where bins are stored and robots operate
- **Robot (R5+):** The automated machines that move on top of the grid and retrieve bins
- **Bin:** Plastic containers that hold inventory items
- **Port:** Your workstation where bins are presented for picking
- **Controller:** The central computer that manages all robots and tasks
- **Pick:** Removing an item from a bin to fulfill an order
- **Replenishment:** Adding new inventory to bins
- **Digging:** When a robot must move bins on top to reach a deeper bin

### Your Role
As an operator, you:
- Pick items from bins as instructed by the workstation screen
- Replenish bins with new inventory
- Process returns
- Report any issues with bins, items, or the system
- Follow safety procedures at all times

## Module 2: Workstation Operation

### Starting Your Shift
1. Log in to the workstation terminal with your operator ID
2. Verify the port is in "ready" status (green indicator)
3. Check that the scanner is working (scan the test barcode on the terminal)
4. Confirm your pick assignment on the terminal screen

### Picking Workflow
1. A bin arrives at your port (green light turns on)
2. The screen shows which item(s) to pick and the quantity
3. Pick the item from the bin
4. Scan the item barcode to confirm
5. Place the item in the shipping container indicated on screen
6. Press "Confirm" on the terminal
7. The bin automatically returns to the grid
8. The next bin arrives (typically within 15-30 seconds)

### What To Do If...

**The wrong bin arrives:**
- Press "Wrong Bin" on the terminal
- Do NOT pick any items
- The bin will be returned and the correct one requested

**An item is missing from the bin:**
- Press "Item Not Found" on the terminal
- Check around and under other items in the bin
- If still not found, confirm "Missing" — the system will update inventory

**An item is damaged:**
- Press "Damaged Item" on the terminal
- Place the damaged item in the quarantine bin (red bin beside your station)
- The system will update inventory and create a replacement pick if needed

**The screen shows an error:**
- Note the error code
- Press the "Help" button to notify the supervisor
- Do not restart the terminal unless instructed

### Ergonomic Tips
- Adjust your workstation height so you can reach into bins without excessive bending
- Rotate between picking and replenishment tasks every 2 hours if possible
- Use the footrest provided at standing stations
- Report any ergonomic discomfort to your supervisor — workstations are adjustable

## Module 3: Replenishment

### When Replenishment Happens
- New inventory arrives at the warehouse
- The system requests replenishment when a SKU's bin stock is low
- Your supervisor assigns replenishment tasks during non-peak hours

### Replenishment Workflow
1. Select "Replenishment Mode" on the terminal
2. Scan the SKU barcode on the incoming inventory
3. The system delivers the appropriate bin (or an empty bin for new SKUs)
4. Green light: place items in the bin
5. Scan each item as you place it (or enter quantity for bulk replenishment)
6. Press "Complete" when done
7. The bin returns to the grid

### Replenishment Rules
- Never exceed the maximum bin weight (printed on the bin label)
- Do not stack items above the bin rim
- Place heavy items at the bottom
- Use dividers for small items
- If a bin is damaged, press "Replace Bin" to get a new one

## Module 4: Returns Processing

### Returns Workflow
1. Select "Returns Mode" on the terminal
2. Scan the return authorization barcode
3. Inspect the returned item per the quality checklist on screen
4. Scan the item barcode
5. Select the item condition: "Like New," "Good," "Damaged," or "Defective"
6. If returnable: the system requests the home bin; place the item in it
7. If not returnable: place in the quarantine bin

### Quality Inspection Quick Guide
- **Like New:** Item unopened or in original condition
- **Good:** Item opened but fully functional, minor cosmetic wear
- **Damaged:** Physical damage but may be resellable at discount
- **Defective:** Non-functional, cannot be resold

## Module 5: Safety at the Workstation

### Emergency Stop
Every workstation has a red E-STOP mushroom button. Press it if:
- A bin appears unstable or is about to fall
- You hear unusual sounds from the port mechanism
- You see smoke, sparks, or smell burning
- Someone is in danger

After pressing E-STOP:
- Your port stops immediately
- Robots near your port halt
- A supervisor is automatically notified
- Do NOT resume operation — wait for a supervisor or technician

### Port Safety Rules
- Never reach into the port mechanism (the area below the bin presentation point)
- Wait for the green light before touching a bin
- Keep hands clear of the bin when pressing "Confirm" (the bin may move immediately)
- Do not lean into the port opening
- Report any unusual sounds or movements from the port mechanism

### General Safety Reminders
- Keep your work area clean and free of tripping hazards
- Store personal items in the designated locker area, not at the workstation
- Take breaks as scheduled — fatigue increases injury risk
- Wear your safety shoes at all times in the warehouse area

## Module 6: Reporting Issues

### How to Report
- **Minor issues** (damaged bin, missing item): Use the terminal "Report" function
- **System issues** (port malfunction, screen error): Press "Help" on the terminal
- **Safety issues** (spill, damaged floor, missing E-STOP sign): Contact supervisor directly
- **Emergencies** (injury, fire, major equipment failure): Press E-STOP, then call emergency line

### What Information to Include
- Your operator ID and workstation number
- Time the issue occurred
- What you observed (be specific)
- What action you took (if any)
- Bin ID or item barcode (if relevant)

## Assessment

After completing this training, you will:
1. Complete a 20-question written assessment (passing score: 80%)
2. Demonstrate picking, replenishment, and returns workflows with a trainer observing
3. Correctly identify and use the E-STOP button
4. Correctly report a simulated issue using the terminal

Successful completion grants you Operator Level 1 certification, valid for 12 months.
